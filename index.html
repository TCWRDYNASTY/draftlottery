How the Lottery Works

Suspense builds as the draft order is revealed in reverse, from Pick #8 down to Pick #1.

  • Reverse Reveal: Once a team is drawn, it receives the current pick and is removed from the lottery.
  • High Odds: Teams with higher weights are more likely to be drawn early for Pick #8.
  • The Jackpot: A low-odds team that survives the early rounds gains a much stronger chance at Pick #1.
